What vHuman does, who it works with, and how a project starts.

What is vHuman?

vHuman is an American creative technology studio specializing in software, interfaces, 3D, and spatial computing. Our work brings design and engineering together to turn complex technology into products people understand, trust, and enjoy.

Is vHuman the same as vHuman Studios?

Yes. vHuman, also known as vHuman Studios, is the creative technology studio at vhumanstudios.com, separate from Human Studios and other studios with similar names.

What does vHuman do?

vHuman works in four areas: software and digital products, interfaces and design systems, 3D and spatial computing, and research and development. The Services page describes each one with the projects behind it.

Who does vHuman work with?

vHuman works with brands, product teams, aerospace programs, sports organizations, and game studios worldwide.

Where is vHuman based?

vHuman is an American studio. The team works remotely, with clients around the world.

How does a project with vHuman work?

One team designs and builds, from first concept to shipped product, so work launches sooner, with fewer handoffs. The tools the studio makes for itself, Studio Kit 26, are shared with the teams it works with, so a project runs on one pipeline from start to finish.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on the scope of each project, so there is no price list. Describe what you want to make in an email, and the conversation about scope and price starts there.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the scope, and it is worked out together once the project is clear. If a date cannot move, say so in the first email.

How do I start a project?

Write to eve@vhumanstudios.com with what you're making and where it runs, who it's for, where it stands, when you need it, your budget, and links to anything that already exists. The Contact page has the full list.

Can vHuman find out whether an idea is possible?

Yes. When the question is whether something can be done, the studio researches it, builds what it needs to find out, and reports back with what it learned.

What is Studio Kit 26?

Studio Kit 26 is vHuman’s in-house toolset: Atlas, Spare Time Dynamics, Flow, and Spatial Capture. It is shared with the partners and clients the studio works with, as part of a project, and is not sold on its own.

Which 3D formats does vHuman work with?

AR work centers on USDZ, the format Apple devices use for augmented reality, including optimizing and inspecting models before they ship. Complex source models are rebuilt in Blender into clean, editable assets for real-time rendering, AR, and VR.

Is vHuman related to virtual humans or Dungeons & Dragons?

The name comes from where the studio started, designing 3D virtual humans for VR, AR, and games; the v is for virtual. vHuman has no connection to the Variant Human of Dungeons & Dragons or to research groups named virtual human.
