Frequently asked questions

What is vHuman?

vHuman is an American creative technology studio specializing in software, interfaces, 3D, and spatial computing. Design and engineering sit on one team.

What does the name mean?

The “v” is for virtual. The studio started by designing 3D virtual humans for VR, AR, and games, and the name stayed. vHuman and vHuman Studios are the same studio. We are not related to Human Studios, to Dungeons & Dragons’ Variant Human, or to research groups that use the term “virtual human.”

What does vHuman do?

vHuman works across research and development, software and digital products, interfaces and design systems, and 3D and spatial computing. Each one is described on Services.

Can I hire vHuman for just one service?

Yes. Most projects use two or three, but research, interface design, and 3D work are each hired on their own.

Who does vHuman work with?

Brands, product teams, aerospace programs, sports organizations, and game studios worldwide. Founders and small teams are welcome too.

Where is vHuman based?

The United States. The team works remotely and clients are around the world.

How does a project with vHuman work?

  • We talk it through. Send what you have, however rough. We come back with how we’d approach it and a written scope before work starts.
  • We prove the hard part. The riskiest piece gets built first and put on the real device, so you find out early whether it holds up.
  • We build in the open. Design and engineering run side by side, and you see working builds along the way, not a reveal at the end.
  • We ship and hand over. We launch it and hand over the code, files, and documentation.

Our in-house tools are part of every project, so the whole thing runs on one pipeline. They are not sold separately.

Who owns the work when it’s done?

You do. Code, source files, 3D models, and documentation are yours at handover.

Can vHuman find out whether an idea is possible?

Yes. That is what research and development is for. We study the feasibility, build what we need to test it, and give you a clear answer with the prototype to back it.

What platforms and formats does vHuman build for?

Web, iOS, macOS, and visionOS. For 3D, we focus on USDZ for AR on Apple devices, including optimizing and inspecting models, and rebuild complex models in Blender as clean, editable assets for real-time rendering, AR, and VR.

How much does a project cost?

There is no price list. Cost depends on scope, and we give you a number after you describe the project by email. Including your budget in that email gets you a faster, straighter answer.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on scope and is set once the project is defined. If you have a fixed deadline, say so in the first email.

How do I start a project?

Email eve@vhumanstudios.com. Say what you want to make and where it runs, who it’s for, where it stands (with links to anything that exists), when you need it, and your budget. Contact has the full list.
