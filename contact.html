What to include in your email

  • What you're making and where it runs. A website, an iOS app, an AR experience for Vision Pro or a trade show floor. The platform settles many of the technical choices before design starts.
  • Who it's for. The people who will use it, and what they need to get done.
  • Where it stands. An idea, a sketch, a working prototype, or a live product that needs a rebuild. We work at every stage; this tells us where to start.
  • When you need it. A launch, a demo, a conference, a funding round. A real date shapes the scope, and if there's no deadline, that's useful to know too.
  • The budget you're working with. An estimate is enough for us to tell you if we're the right fit.
  • Links to anything that exists. A repo, a Figma file, a site, a video.

A few paragraphs are enough. You don't need a finished brief.

Not sure what you need? Services and Work show what we do, and the FAQ answers the usual questions. Or just write, and we'll work it out together.
