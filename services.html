Our work brings design and engineering together to create technology that is useful, thoughtful, and enjoyable to use.

vHuman works across research and development, software and digital products, interfaces and design systems, and 3D and spatial computing. Research shows what’s possible, design decides how it looks and how people use it, and engineering makes it work. Everything happens with one team under one direction, so nothing gets lost in handoffs and you talk to the same people from first idea to launch.

What we do

Research and development

We find out whether an idea can work before you fund it.

  • Feasibility studies
  • Proofs of concept
  • Rapid prototyping
  • Technical research and audits
  • Emerging technology exploration
  • AI and machine learning experiments
  • Computer vision and sensor input

Software and digital products

We design and build products end to end, along with the systems behind them.

  • Web applications
  • iOS and mobile apps
  • SaaS platforms
  • Internal tools and dashboards
  • APIs and backend systems
  • Sign-in, accounts, and user management
  • AI and machine learning features
  • Data visualization
  • Rebuilds of existing products

Interfaces and design systems

We design the interface and the system behind it, so the next feature looks like it belongs.

  • UI and UX design
  • Product design
  • Design systems
  • Component libraries
  • Websites
  • Prototypes and user flows
  • Motion and interaction design
  • Typography and visual identity
  • Accessibility

3D and spatial computing

We build 3D for the device it runs on, at the frame rate the platform demands.

  • AR experiences
  • VR experiences
  • Apple Vision Pro and visionOS apps
  • Real-time 3D for web, apps, and games
  • 3D modeling and asset rebuilds
  • 3D optimization and performance
  • USDZ, glTF, and AR Quick Look
  • LiDAR scanning and spatial capture
  • Product visualization and configurators
  • 3D environments and maps

Not every project needs all four. The FAQ answers the usual questions, or write and we’ll work out together where to start.
